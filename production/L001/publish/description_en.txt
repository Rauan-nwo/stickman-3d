How a woman US officials suspect of working for Chinese intelligence got close to Jensen Huang, the man who ran Nvidia, and why nobody warned him.

Alice Huang ran Megaspeed, a Singapore company whose assets jumped from $33 million to about $3 billion in a single year, with servers full of Nvidia chips sitting in Malaysia. For more than a year, American intelligence listened to her calls, wrote a memo and briefed the White House. Nobody told the man at the table.

Who should have warned him?
